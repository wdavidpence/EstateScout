British Hallmark System

Lion Passant = 925 sterling. Date letters = year (font + shield shape = specific year). Maker marks = two initials in shield. Duty marks = monarch profile (1784-1890).

📅 Pre-1965 Rule

Identification Checklist

  • Check date — pre-1965 = silver (mostly)
  • Look for mint marks (D, S, O, CC, W)
  • Edge should be reeded (grooved), not smooth
  • Weight and size should match specifications
  • 1943 silver pennies exist (extremely rare)

💡 Estate Sale Coin Tips

Check any jar of mixed coins for pre-1965 dimes/quarters. Look for coin albums. Morgan dollars are the most collectible. British crowns are often overlooked.

Gemstone Identification

💎

Diamond

Hardest gem (Mohs 10). Check for GIA/AGS certificates. Even small diamonds in silver settings can be valuable.

🔴

Ruby

Deep red (pigeon blood most valuable). Mohs 9. Old mine cuts are collectible.

🔵

Sapphire

Blue (also pink, yellow). Mohs 9. Old cushion and oval cuts are collectible.

💚

Emerald

Green with jardin inclusions. Mohs 7.5-8. Often oil-treated. Colombian most valuable.

🌈

Opal

Play-of-color (rainbow flash). Soft (5.5-6.5). Australian black opal most valuable.

⚪

Pearl

Warm to touch (not cold like glass). Akoya, South Sea, Tahitian most valuable.

🟤

Garnet

Deep red/burgundy. Mohs 6.5-7.5. Common in Victorian jewelry.

🟠

Amber

Warm golden. May contain fossils. Baltic amber most collectible.

🟢

Turquoise

Blue-green with matrix. Native American silver settings (Navajo, Zuni) are collectible.

Antique Periods

Georgian (1714-1837)

Hand-made, rose-cut diamonds, paste jewelry.

Victorian (1837-1901)

Closed back settings, foil backing, cameos. Very collectible.

Edwardian (1901-1915)

Lace-like filigree, platinum, old European cuts.

Art Deco (1920-1935)

Geometric designs, onyx, jade, baguette cuts. Highly collectible.

Retro (1940-1955)

Bold, oversized, gold, colored stones.

💎 Value Tips

Pairs are essential for earrings. Complete sets worth more. Famous maker marks add 5-10x value. Antique periods command premiums. Check for certificates.

Category Tips

📮 Stamps

Look for first editions, error stamps, rare country issues. Check perforations and watermarks. Complete sets worth more than singles.

📖 Rare Books

First editions, signed copies, historical significance. Check dust jackets, edition statements, and bookplates. Early American imprints valuable.

🏺 Antiques

Pre-1900 items with maker marks. Check for period-appropriate materials and construction. Original finish adds value.

🎨 Decorative Art

Art Nouveau and Arts & Crafts pieces command premiums. Look for maker marks, originality, and condition. Tiffany, Liberty, Moravka.

📷 Vintage Cameras

Leica, Hasselblad, Contax, Rolleifex. Working condition essential. Original cases and accessories add value.

🎸 Musical Instruments

Branded instruments (Martin, Gibson, Fender). Check for original hardware, condition, and playability. Vintage amplifiers collectible.

💡 Estate Sale Collectible Tips

Check basements and attics for old collections. Look for original boxes, certificates, and documentation. Complete sets always worth more. When in doubt, research before buying.
