60-Second Checklist

  • ✓
    Look for marks: 925/Sterling = solid | EPNS/Plated = not
  • ✓
    Check weight: Heavy for size = likely solid
  • !
    Check wear points: Yellow/copper = plated
  • ✓
    Magnet test: Sticks = NOT silver
  • ✓
    Make decision: Solid + good = BUY

💡 Pro Tip

Focus on HEAVY items with NO wear color showing. These are most likely solid silver.
