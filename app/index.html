Fakes & Reproductions

  • !
    Shallow stamping — fake hallmarks
  • !
    Wrong font on antique items
  • !
    Perfectly identical marks = reproduction
  • ✗
    Magnet sticks = NOT silver
  • !
    Weighted silver (lead-filled)
